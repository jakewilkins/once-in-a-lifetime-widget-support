# Once In A Lifetime Widget — Support

Welcome! This is the official support page for the **Once In A Lifetime Widget** iOS app. If you're having trouble with the app or have a question, you're in the right place.

---

## 📋 How to Submit a Support Ticket

1. Click the **[Issues](../../issues)** tab at the top of this page (or [click here](../../issues/new/choose)).
2. Click **New Issue**.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** – A short, descriptive summary of your problem (e.g., *"Widget doesn't update on Lock Screen"*).
   - **Description** – Include as much detail as possible:
     - What you expected to happen
     - What actually happened
     - Steps to reproduce the issue
     - Your iPhone model and iOS version (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4)
     - App version (found in the App Store → Updates, or Settings → General → iPhone Storage)
     - Any screenshots or screen recordings that help illustrate the problem
4. Click **Submit new issue**.

We'll do our best to respond as quickly as possible. Thank you for your patience!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: The widget isn't showing up on my Home Screen or Lock Screen. What do I do?**
A: Try removing the widget and re-adding it. Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap the **+** button, search for *Once In A Lifetime Widget*, and add it again.

**Q: The widget data looks outdated or isn't refreshing. What should I check?**
A: iOS controls how often widgets refresh to save battery. Make sure Background App Refresh is enabled for the app: go to **Settings → General → Background App Refresh** and ensure *Once In A Lifetime Widget* is turned on.

**Q: The app crashed. How do I report it?**
A: Please [open a new issue](../../issues/new/choose) and include your device model, iOS version, and app version. If you have a crash log (from **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data**), attaching it is very helpful.

**Q: I purchased the app but lost access after switching phones. How do I restore?**
A: Open the app and look for a **Restore Purchases** option in the app's Settings screen. If that doesn't work, please [open a support ticket](../../issues/new/choose).

**Q: How do I find my app version?**
A: Open the **App Store**, tap your profile icon (top right), scroll down to **Updated Recently**, and find *Once In A Lifetime Widget*. The version number is listed there. Alternatively, go to **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Once In A Lifetime Widget**.

**Q: I have a feature request. Where should I submit it?**
A: We'd love to hear your ideas! Please [open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and use **Feature Request** as the title prefix (e.g., *"Feature Request: Dark mode support"*).
